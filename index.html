RACC & ROLL

Racc & Roll is a co-op metal detecting hangout game. As a lil raccoon, you metal detect, explore, relax, and collect all the trash and treasure hidden in the ground. Wishlist on Steam

Read More

Use your metal detector and upgrades to find and dig up 500+ unique collectables across the island. Some come with short stories and events to uncover. Build and decorate your own hidden base with the trash you like, or build one together with your buddies.

Play solo or online with friends. Explore the island and hang out at your own pace, unlocking and collecting cosmetics to customize your character.

VESSEL

Vessel is a 3D puzzle game that invites you to explore the ruins of an ancient civilization that once wielded the power of Water.

Read More

Guided by a network of static cameras, you'll navigate intricate environments filled with secrets waiting to be uncovered.

You play as a broken pot, resurrected by a mystical water spirit and granted the extraordinary ability to SWAP-a power that lets your exchange places of the water spirit with pieces in your surroundings. With this newfound ability, embark on a journey to reclaim your missing pieces and unravel the forgotten mysteries of a world shaped by water.
